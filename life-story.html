How It Works

Four Steps. No Pressure.

You don't need to prepare anything. You don't need to know what to say. Just show up and talk.

Open the Page

On your phone, tablet, or computer. You'll see a simple page with a Start button and a microphone permission. That's all.

Talk

The interviewer asks questions about your life — where you grew up, who raised you, what you loved, what you survived. You answer in your own words.

We Listen

Every word is recorded. The interviewer follows your lead — if you go deep on something, they stay there. No rushing, no moving on before you're ready.

Get Your Story

After the interview, we transcribe, edit, and deliver your PDF, ePub, and audio. You get everything. It's yours. You can resume anytime, and your data is yours to delete whenever you choose.

Your Data

We Don't Want Your Data

Your interview is yours. Here's what that means in practice.

🔒

Local Transcription

Your audio is transcribed on your device. Only the text of what you say is sent to our server — never the audio itself.

🚧

Delete Anytime

You can delete your interview data from our server at any time — before, during, or after the interview. One tap, it's gone.

🕑

Auto-Delete

After your deliverables are complete, we delete our server copy within 30 days. No keeping your story "just in case."
